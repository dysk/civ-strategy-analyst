# LEKMOD civilizations (34.15)

Unique abilities, units, buildings and improvements per civilization.
"(unchanged)" means identical to the base game (Brave New World).

## Akkad (Sargon)
- **Ability — The Great Unification:** +3 Culture, +2 Happiness and +1 Production from conquered cities. Great Generals grant nearby Units a +15% Combat Bonus when initiating combat with enemy Cities.
- **Building — Akkadian Library:** Replaces the Library. Provides +1 additional Science and does not require Gold maintenance. Costs less to produce.
- **Unit — Laputtu:** Replaces Spearmen. 3 Movement. Units that start their turn on the same tile as this unit copy this unit’s Movement stat. Ability is kept on upgrade.
- **Bias:** River

## Aksum (Ezana)
- **Ability — Saint Elesbaan's Blessing:** Upon founding a Religion, your cities immediately follow it. Your religion does not exert Religious pressure on foreign cities.
- **Improvement — Rock-Hewn Church:** Available at Mining. Provides +2 Faith. +1 Faith at Theology. May not be built adjacent to each other nor on resource tiles. Quarries adjacent to this improvement yield +1 Faith for each adjacent. Units healing on or next to this improvement yield +2 Faith for the unit owner.
- **Building — King Ezana’s Stele:** Replaces the National Epic. Provides +3 Culture and +4 Faith in addition to its base yields. All monuments in the empire provide an additional +1 Food, +1 Production, +1 Gold and +1 Faith.
- **Bias:** Hill

## America (Washington)
- **Ability — Manifest Destiny:** Military Land Units gain +1 Sight. Gold costs for purchasing territory are halved.
- **Unit — Minuteman:** Replaces Musketmen. Ignores terrain costs, arrives trained with Drill I (+15% bonus in Rough terrain), and earns 100% of foe’s Strength as points towards a Golden Age from kills. All abilities are kept on upgrade.
- **Unit — Pioneer:** Replaces the Settler. Has the same combat and movement properties as the Scout (preventing instant capture) and may Settle.
- **Bias:** None

## Arabia (Harun al-Rashid)
- **Ability — Ships of the Desert:** Luxury Resources are improved twice as fast. +2 Gold and Culture from Breweries, Censer Makers, Gem Cutters, Grocers, Mints, and Textile Mills. Receive twice as many Oil resources
- **Unit — Camel Archer:** Replaces the Knight. A Mounted Ranged Unit with 20 Ranged Strength and 14 Combat Strength. Receives no penalty when initiating combat with an enemy City.
- **Building — Bazaar:** Replaces the Market. Provides an additional happiness. Additionally every luxury resource worked by the city provides +1 Gold.
- **Bias:** Desert, avoid wetlands

## Argentina (Eva Perón)
- **Ability — Pride of the People:** Construct Pastures and Farms twice as fast.
- **Unit — Gaucho:** Replaces the Knight. Receives a 50% chance to withdraw from Melee attacks. Flanking bonuses are tripled. Capable of building Pastures and Farms. Abilities are lost on upgrade.
- **Building — Ocupada Estable:** Gain +1 Food from Pasture resources and +1 Production from Maize in addition to the regular benefits of the Stable.
- **Bias:** None

## Armenia (Tiridates III)
- **Ability — Splendor of the Caucasus:** +1 Food, +2 Production and +1 Gold from Mountains (including mountainous Natural Wonders - i.e. Kilimanjaro, Uluru, Mt. Fuji, Mt. Sinai, Mt. Kailash, Rock of Gibraltar and Sri Pada).
- **Unit — Sparapet:** Replaces Horsemen. Requires 80 Production and 2 Horse resources. Boasts a mighty 17 Combat Strength and grants the Great General Combat Bonus to nearby allies. Does not obsolete. Keeps the ability on upgrade and only costs 1 strategic resource.
- **Building — Darbas:** Replaces the Observatory. In addition to the typical perks, provides +4 Culture in the City and +2 Culture on Mountain tiles and Wonders for a total of +5 Science and +2 Culture on those tiles.
- **Bias:** Hill, Avoid Jungle

## Assyria (Ashurbanipal)
- **Ability — Siege Warfare:** Siege Units receive +1 Movement. Receive a free Great Writer at Philosophy, and receive a free Great Work of Writing upon completion of the Royal Library in the Capital.
- **Building — Royal Library:** Replaces the Library. Provides an additional +1 Culture and Science and provides +15 XP to Units trained in the City when its Great Work of Writing slot is filled.
- **Unit — Siege Tower:** Replaces the Catapult. A Melee Siege Unit with 12 Strength only capable of combat with enemy Cities. Arrives trained with Cover I (+33% bonus defending against Ranged attacks) and Extra sight 1. When adjacent to an enemy City, Units within 2 tiles of the Siege Tower gain a +50% Combat Bonus when initiating combat with that City. Keeps Extra sight and Cover I promotion on upgrade.
- **Bias:** Avoid Tundra

## Australia (Henry Parker)
- **Ability — Dreamtime:** +5 Faith from Natural Wonders. Gain 10 Faith upon discovering a Natural Wonder.
- **Unit — Ngangkari:** Replaces the Worker. 3 Movement, receives an additional +1 Movement while embarked, and arrives with Medic I and II (adjacent allies heal an additional +10 HP while Fortified).
- **Building — Convict Penitentiary:** Replaces the Constabulary. Available at Machinery (instead of Banking). In addition to slowing the rate of Technology theft, provides +1 Gold and Production, and +5% Gold and Production in the City. Requires no Gold maintenance and is appreciably cheaper to construct.
- **Bias:** Coast

## Austria (Maria Theresa)
- **Ability — Diplomatic Marriage:** May spend Gold to annex a City-State that has been your ally for at least 5 turns.
- **Unit — Hussar:** Replaces Cavalry. +1 Sight and Movement and is 50% more effective at Flank attacks (bonus from each ally adjacent to foe increases from +10% to +15% per ally). Keeps all abilities on upgrade.
- **Building — Coffee House:** Replaces the Windmill. Available at Printing Press (instead of Economics). In addition to the typical perks, provides a +5% Production bonus and a +25% Great Person generation bonus in the City. Much cheaper to construct.
- **Bias:** Hill

## Ayyubids (Saladin)
- **Ability — Justice of Saladin:** Receive a free Burial Tomb in each City you conquer. Workers construct improvements +25% faster. (The Burial Tomb is a unique building of Egypt that replaces the Temple. If the Temple in a captured City survives, the City will house both buildings.)
- **Building — Madrasah:** Replaces the University. In addition to the typical perks, provides +2 Faith in the City; +1 Science from Flood Plains, +2 Science from Oases.
- **Unit — Mamluk:** Replaces the Knight. Gains a +50% bonus vs. Melee units, +25% vs. Gunpowder units. Slightly more expensive to produce. Keeps the ability on upgrade.
- **Bias:** Desert

## Aztecs (Montezuma)
- **Ability — Sacrificial Captives:** Earn 100% of foe’s Strength as Culture from kills.
- **Building — Floating Gardens:** Replaces the Watermill. In addition to the typical perks, provides +10% Food in the City and each Lake tile in the City provides an additional +1 Food. In addition to Rivers, the Floating Gardens may also be constructed when adjacent to Lakes. Costs less Gold maintenance.
- **Unit — Jaguar:** Replaces the Warrior. Gains a +33% Combat Bonus in Forests and Jungles, heals 25 HP from kills, and moves unimpeded through Forests and Jungles. (These promotions are retained upon upgrade.) Keeps all abilities on upgrade.
- **Bias:** Jungle

## Babylon (Nebuchadnezzar II)
- **Ability — Ingenuity:** Receive a free Great Scientist in the Capital when you discover Philosophy (previously Writing). Earn Great Scientists +25% faster.
- **Building — Walls of Babylon:** Replaces Walls. Boosts City Strength by +6 and raises City health by +100. Cheaper to construct.
- **Unit — Bowman:** Replaces the Archer. 7 Melee Strength and 9 Ranged Strength.
- **Bias:** Avoid Tundra

## Belgium (Leopold II)
- **Ability — Colonialist Riches:** +1 Production from Plantations. +1 Gold from Strategic Resources.
- **Building — Stade:** Replaces Zoo. In addition to the typical perks, provides an additional +1 Happiness, Culture, and Gold, and does not require Gold maintenance. Cheaper to construct. Does not require a Colosseum to be built.
- **Unit — Force Publique:** Replaces Great War Infantry. 51 Strength, arrives with Drill I (+15% bonus in Rough terrain) and Charge (+33% bonus vs. wounded Units). Promotions are kept on upgrade.
- **Bias:** None

## Boers (Stephanus Johannes Paulus Kruger)
- **Ability — The Great Trek:** Farms yield +1 Food when adjacent to at least 2 other Farms. This bonus is increased by +1 Gold at 3, +1 Production at 4, and +1 Culture and Science at 5 adjacent Farms.
- **Building — Staatsmuseum:** Replaces the Opera House. Provides +1 additional Culture and houses an Artist Specialist slot. Cheaper to construct.
- **Unit — Voortrekker:** Replaces Great War Infantry. Gains a +25% Bonus when foes initiate combat against this Unit. Heals completely from kills. Capable of building Farms in 2 turns. Slightly cheaper to construct. Keeps the defense ability and still heals completely from kills on upgrade.
- **Bias:** None

## Bolivia (Tata Belzu)
- **Ability — Revolución Nacional:** Expending a Great Artist improves the yield of Mines with +1 Production and expending a Great Writer improves them with +1 Food instead. (When one yield has already been chosen, choosing the opposite will swap them instead of stacking. This ability cannot be triggered by unique Artist or Writer replacements.)
- **Great Person — Comparsa Folklorica:** Replaces the Great Musician. Moves further and may be consumed to spread Tourism, begin a Golden Age, or gain a large amount of Culture.
- **Unit — Colorado:** Replaces the Anti-Tank Rifle. 40 Strength. Available at Dynamite. Only receives a +100% Bonus vs. Armored Units, but gains +2 Strength for every 5 points of excess Happiness in the empire. (Rounds up. Updates when a unit is moved or when a new turn begins.) Loses ability on upgrade.
- **Bias:** Hill

## Brazil (Pedro II)
- **Ability — Carnival!:** Output 100% more Tourism and generate Great Artists, Writers, and Musicians +100% faster during Golden Ages. Earn 100% of foe’s Strength as Golden Age Points from kills
- **Unit — Pracinha:** Replaces Infantry. 80 Strength. Gains a +20% Combat Bonus outside of friendly territory. May move after attacking. Keeps abilities on upgrade.
- **Improvement — Brazilwood Camp:** Available at Bronze Working (instead of Machinery). May be constructed on Jungle tiles. Provides +1 Production. Gains +2 Gold at Machinery and +2 Culture at Acoustics and improves any resources on the tile.
- **Bias:** Jungle

## Brunei (Bolkiah)
- **Ability — Sea Nomads:** All Naval Units may heal outside friendly territory and pay 33% less Gold maintenance. Melee Naval Units can create improvements on Coast and Ocean tiles.
- **Building — BMPC Plant:** Replaces Oil Refineries. In addition to the typical perks, provides +3 Gold and +5 Production. Doesn’t require nearby Oil to build. Upon completion, provides 2 Oil resources.
- **Improvement — Kampong Ayer:** Requires Optics. May be built on any Coastal tile adjacent to land tiles. Provides +1 Food, Culture and Gold. +1 Production at Navigation, and +1 additional Culture at Flight.
- **Bias:** Coast

## Bulgaria (Asparukh Khan)
- **Ability — Cyrillic Script:** Writer Specialists provide +5 Science; Science buildings provide +1 Culture.
- **Unit — Konnitsa:** Replaces the Knight. Similar to an Impi, performs a Ranged attack when initiating combat. Ability is lost on upgrade.
- **Building — Khambar:** Replaces Granary. Cheaper to construct. In addition to the typical perks, yields +1 Gold from Wheat and Cattle resources.
- **Bias:** None

## Burma (Anawrahta)
- **Ability — Pyu City States:** +1 Happiness global happiness per city.
- **Unit — Aahkyayhkya Sai:** Replaces the settler. The Aahkyayhkya Sai is 40% cheaper to construct, but removes a population in the city when finished. It requires the city to have at least 3 population.
- **Building — Kyawwat:** Replaces Stoneworks. In addition to the typical perks, the Kyawwat extends its yield bonuses to Salt and Iron resources and does not require any resources within the City to construct.
- **Bias:** River

## Byzantium (Theodora)
- **Ability — Patriarchate of Constantinople (unchanged):** Choose an additional Pantheon, Follower, Founder or Enhancer Belief when founding a Religion.
- **Building — Hippodrome:** Replaces Amphitheater. Slightly less costly to produce. In addition to the typical perks, also provides +2 Faith and +1 Happiness in the City.
- **Unit — Cataphract (unchanged):** Replaces Horsemen. 15 Strength, but 3 Movement. Able to receive defensive Terrain Bonuses and is penalized slightly less for attacking Cities (-25% vs. -33%). Abilities are lost on upgrade.
- **Bias:** None (no longer Coastal)

## Canada (John A. MacDonald)
- **Ability — Canadian Fur Trade:** +2 Gold from Camps, +2 Gold and +1 Culture from Lake tiles.
- **Unit — Combat Engineer:** Replaces Rifleman. Available at Industrialization. Identical Strength, but +1 Movement. May construct Roads, Railroads and Forts, and clear Forests, Jungles and Marsh tiles. Abilities are lost on upgrade.
- **Building — Tim Horton’s:** Replaces Stock Exchange. In addition to the typical perks, provides +2 Happiness in the City and +1 Gold to river tiles worked by this City. Significantly cheaper to construct.
- **Bias:** None

## Carthage (Dido)
- **Ability — Phoenecian Heritage:** All Coastal Cities receive a free Harbor. Units may cross mountains, receiving 50 HP damage if they end their turn on one. (The ability to cross mountains is no longer tied to earning a Great General). (Note: Harbors will only form City Connections after the discovery of the Wheel, but will still provide the appropriate Gold.)
- **Building — Cothon:** Replaces Lighthouse. +1 Food and +1 Production in the City in addition to the typical perks.
- **Unit — African Forest Elephant:** Replaces Horsemen, but does not require Horses to construct. 14 Strength, but 3 Movement. Possesses the Feared Elephant (enemy Units adjacent to this Unit receive a -10% combat penalty), Great Generals II (earns Great General points faster) promotion and Hannibal’s Charge (+20% Strength when initiating combat from a higher elevation than foe; enemy Units will retreat if they receive more damage than this Unit, this Unit deals +50% damage to defenders incapable of retreat). Loses the feared Elephant ability, but keeps Great Generals II and Hannibal’s Charge on upgrade. (Feared Elephant will not stack.)
- **Bias:** Coast

## Celts (Boudicca)
- **Ability — Druidic Lore:** Cities with an adjacent unimproved Forest tile generate +1 Faith, increasing to +2 Faith with three adjacent tiles.
- **Building — Silversmith:** Replaces the Mint. Provides +2 Production and +1 Gold and does not require resources to construct, but retains the typical perks of a Mint. Less costly to produce.
- **Unit — Pictish Warrior:** Replaces Spearmen. Earn 100% of your foe’s Combat Strength as Faith from kills (previously 50%). Gains a +20% Combat Bonus outside of friendly territory and may pillage without movement penalties. Keeps the abilities on upgrade.
- **Bias:** Forest

## Chile (Bernardo O’ Higgens)
- **Ability — By Reason or By Force:** Claim all surrounding neutral tiles upon the completion of Fishing Boat or Drydock improvements. Receive a free Great Admiral at Compass. Friendly Melee & Gunpowder Units inflict a -25% Combat Penalty to adjacent enemy Naval Units; Melee Naval Units inflict this penalty to adjacent enemy Land Units. (Embarked Units do not apply these penalties.)
- **Building — Cooperative:** Replaces the Factory. In addition to the typical perks, provides +2 Happiness and houses an additional Engineer Specialist. Does not require Gold maintenance. Ideologies are unlocked for Chile after the completion of only 1 Cooperative (compared to 3 Factories).
- **Unit — Cardoen:** Replaces the Helicopter Gunship. Stronger. In addition to the typical bonuses, the Cardoen is capable of attacking and capturing Cities, has 2 additional movement, and receives a +100% Combat Bonus defending against ranged attacks.
- **Bias:** Coast

## China (Wu Zetian)
- **Ability — Art of War (unchanged):** Great Generals provide an additional +15% Combat Bonus to nearby Units and are generated +50% faster.
- **Building — Paper Maker (unchanged):** Replaces the Library. Requires no Gold maintenance and provides +2 Gold.
- **Unit — Chu-Ko-Nu (unchanged):** Replaces Crossbowmen. Weaker at base, but may attack twice each turn. Ability is kept on upgrade.
- **Bias:** None

## Colombia (Simon Bolivar)
- **Ability — Independence of the People:** +1 Food from Lumber Mills. Lumber Mills receive an additional +1 Science at Chemistry.
- **Building — Cacicazgo:** Replaces the Granary. In addition to the typical perks, provides +1 Production and allows the City to send Production through Trade Routes (as a Workshop would). Doesn’t require Gold maintenance. Slightly more expensive to construct.
- **Unit — Llanero:** Replaces Cavalry. Capable of receiving defensive terrain bonuses and Fortifying. Ability is lost on upgrade.
- **Bias:** Forest, Plains

## Cuba (Fidel Castro)
- **Ability — ¡Viva la Revolución!:** Receive +1 Culture per turn in your Capital for every 5 Culture per turn generated in the Capital of Civilizations you’ve met. Upon selecting your first Ideology Tenet, receive 2 Guerrilleros in the Capital. Receive -50% less Unhappiness from Ideology pressure.
- **Unit — Guerrillero:** Replaces Great War Infantry. Available to produce only after the adoption of Cuba’s first Ideology Tenet (as opposed to Replaceable Parts). Weaker, but boasts 3 Movement and can be built for significantly less. Unit upgrades with normal movement.
- **Building — Dance Hall:** Replaces the Opera House. In addition to the typical perks, the Dance Hall provides +1 Happiness, +4 Culture, and +15% Production towards military Units in the City when its Great Work slot is filled. (This bonus is updated at the start of every turn.) Cheaper to construct.
- **Bias:** None

## Czechia (Vladislav II)
- **Ability — Hussite Fervor:** +100% Religious Pressure towards your own cities. Choose a Reformation belief upon founding a religion.
- **Building — Thaler Mint:** Replaces Mint. In addition to the typical perks, yields +2 Gold from Iron and is also unlocked by it. Costs 25% less production to construct. Has a Great Merchant Specialist Slot.
- **Unit — Czechoslovak Legion:** Replaces Foreign Legion. In addition to the typical perks, it is stronger, does not require a specific policy to be unlocked, and has a 33% combat bonus against military units from civilizations with a different ideology than the player who owns the unit. 33% cheaper to buy with Gold and may move immediately after doing so.
- **Bias:** None

## Denmark (Harald Bluetooth)
- **Ability — Viking Fury:** Embarked Units gain +1 Movement and may disembark for only 1 Movement; Civilian units may also embark for the same cost. Melee Units pillage without movement penalties.
- **Unit — Berserker (unchanged):** Replaces Longswordsmen. Available at Metal Casting instead of Steel. 3 Movement and ignores penalties for attacking over rivers or while embarked. Upgrades to normal movement, but keeps the Amphibious promotion.
- **Unit — Longship:** Replaces Trireme. 6 Movement. Embarked Units that begin their turn on the same tile as a Longship are granted 6 Movement for that turn. (This Movement bonus is retained after disembarkation. Promoted Longships with bonus Movement will also confer this bonus to the embarked Unit.) Ability is kept on upgrade, bonus movement is lost.
- **Bias:** Coast

## Egypt (Ramesses II)
- **Ability — Monument Builders:** +15% Production towards the construction of World Wonders and National Wonders.
- **Building — Burial Tomb (unchanged):** Replaces the Temple. In addition to the typical perks, provides +2 Happiness and does not require Gold maintenance. Cities with a Burial Tomb double the amount of Gold plundered for the conqueror.
- **Unit — War Chariot (unchanged):** Replaces the Chariot Archer. 5 Movement and does not require Horse resources to construct. Upgrades into a normal knight.
- **Bias:** Avoid Forest, Jungle

## England (Elizabeth)
- **Ability — Sun Never Sets:** English Naval Units receive +2 Movement. Receive an additional Spy upon entering the Renaissance. Constabularies and Police Stations are built twice as fast and provide +1 Happiness.
- **Unit — Longbowman:** Replaces Crossbowmen. 12 Melee Strength and 18 Ranged Strength, but wields 3 Range. Range promotion is kept on upgrade.
- **Unit — Ship of the Line:** Replaces the Frigate. +1 Sight. 25 Combat Strength and 30 Ranged Strength. (The Ship of the Line has 5 Movement at base, increasing effectively to 7 due to Sun Never Sets.) Keeps the +1 Sight promotion on upgrade.
- **Bias:** Coast

## Ethiopia (Haile Selassie)
- **Ability — Spirit of Adwa (unchanged):** Ethiopian Units gain a +20% Bonus in combat with Civilizations with more Cities than Ethiopia.
- **Building — Stele (unchanged):** Replaces the Monument. In addition to the Culture yield, provides +2 Faith.
- **Unit — Mehal Sefari:** Replaces Riflemen. 35 Strength. Arrives trained with Drill I (+15% Bonus in Rough terrain) and gains Strength according to distance from the Capital, peaking at +30% when the Mehal Sefari is stationed inside the Capital and decreasing by -3% per tile away. Cheaper to produce. Keeps the ability and the promotion on upgrade.
- **Bias:** None

## Finland (Mannerheim)
- **Ability — Finnish Mobility:** All Melee and Mounted Units receive a +50% increased flanking bonus (+15% per ally adjacent to foe vs. 10%). +1 Culture from unimproved Forest tiles.
- **Unit — Sissi:** Replaces the Machine Gun. Ignores terrain costs and receives a +25% Combat Bonus vs. Armored Units. Has an additional movement and does not spend movement when pillaging tiles, but cannot attack twice. 60 Ranged Strength, 50 Melee Strength. Cheaper to construct. Keeps all abilities and promotions AND is able to attack twice on upgrade.
- **Building — Sauna:** Replaces the Shrine. +2 Culture from Lake tiles. Land and Naval Units trained in Cities with a Sauna heal +10 HP inside friendly territory. Costs no gold maintenance.
- **Bias:** None

## France (Napoleon)
- **Ability — City of Light:** +1 Culture in the Capital. +2 Culture from World Wonders. Theming bonuses are doubled in the Capital.
- **Improvement — Chateau:** Provides +2 Science, +2 Culture and +1 Gold, increasing by +1 Culture and +2 Gold at Flight, and +1 Science after adopting the Free Thought policy. Provides the same defensive bonus as a Fort (+50% bonus when foe initiates combat). Chateaus must be constructed adjacent to a luxury resource, non-adjacent to another Chateau, and not atop an existing Resource tile. (Resources discovered after the construction of the Chateau will provide their yield bonus to the tile, but the Chateau will not ‘connect’ those resources.)
- **Unit — Musketeer (unchanged):** Replaces Musketmen. 28 Strength. That’s it.
- **Bias:** None

## Franks (Charlemagne)
- **Ability — Holy Roman Empire:** +1 Faith from Farms. Frankish Units receive +25% additional Movement when traveling along friendly and neutral Roads and Railroads.
- **Building — Mead Hall:** Replaces the Colosseum. Provides an additional point of Happiness and a bonus +1 Culture. Costs 25% less production to construct. Costs no gold maintenance.
- **Unit — Seaxman:** Replaces Longswordsmen. Available at Chivalry instead of Steel. 22 Strength. Arrives trained with the Cover I (+33% bonus defending against ranged attacks) and Amphibious (attack from the sea or over rivers without penalty) promotions. Almost negligibly cheaper to construct. Promotions are kept on upgrade.
- **Bias:** None

## Gaul (Vercingetorix)
- **Ability — Oppidum of Bibracte:** Receive a permanent Murus Gallicus in the Capital upon the discovery of Mining.
- **Building — Murus Gallicus:** Replaces Walls. Slightly cheaper and better defense (+6 vs. +5). +1 Production and +1 Happiness; +1 additional Production after researching Metal Casting.
- **Unit — Noble Swordsman:** Replaces Longswordsmen. 20 Strength. Gains a +25% Combat Bonus when initiating combat and a +50% Bonus in Forests. Does not require Iron resources. Becomes obsolete at Rifling (instead of Gunpowder) and upgrades into Riflemen. Keeps ability on upgrade.
- **Bias:** Forest

## Georgia (Tamar)
- **Ability — Ecclesiastical Architecture:** Buildings unlocked by religious beliefs (except Houses of Worship) are built using Production instead (cost equal to 50% of the required Faith); these buildings provide +2 Production.
- **Building — Tsikhe:** Replaces Walls. In addition to typical defensive perks, provides +3 Faith, and +1 Culture from Strategic Resources (e.g. Horses, Iron). Units trained in this City earn Golden Age points from kills (if the killed unit is pre-industrial).
- **Unit — Khevsur:** Replaces Swordsmen. Arrives at Guilds technology instead of Iron Working, but ignores terrain movement costs as a Scout would, and receives a +33% Combat Bonus during Golden Ages. Loses ‘ignores terrain movement cost’ on upgrade.
- **Bias:** None

## Germany (Bismarck)
- **Ability — Precision Engineering:** +1 Science and Production from Workshops, Windmills, Factories and Hydro Plants. +33% faster Great Engineer generation.
- **Building — Hanse:** Replaces Banks. In addition to the typical perks, gain +5% Production in the City for every Trade Route your empire currently maintains with a City-State.
- **Unit — Panzer:** Replaces the Tank. 80 Strength and 6 Movement. Loses bonus movement on upgrade.
- **Bias:** None

## Golden Horde (Batu Khan)
- **Ability — Golden Conquest:** Units are +50% more effective at intimidating City-States. Puppeted Cities provide +50% Production, Gold, and Science, and generate -33% less Unhappiness.
- **Building — Yam Route:** Replaces Caravansaries. Available at The Wheel instead of Horseback Riding. Retains Trade Route perks but doesn’t provide Gold, instead providing +3 Science. Allows Airlifting between Cities with Yam Routes.
- **Unit — Ulan:** Replaces Pikemen. Stronger than its counterpart. Behaves as a Mounted Unit and requires a Horse resource. 4 Movement. Arrives with Charge (+33% bonus against wounded foes) and does not receive a penalty in combat with Cities. Keeps Charge promotion on upgrade.
- **Bias:** None

## Goths (Alaric I)
- **Ability — Drauhtinon:** Melee and Gunpowder units receive +1 Movement.
- **Unit — Gadrauht:** Replaces Longswordsman. 22 Strength. Cheaper to produce and does not require Iron to train. Heals 25HP upon killing a unit. Keeps ability on upgrade.
- **Improvement — Harjis:** Available at Mining. Must be adjacent to a Luxury resource (but not on a Luxury resource) and non-adjacent to another Harjis. Provides +1 Production. Gains an additional +1 Production upon the discovery of Iron Working, Gunpowder and Dynamite.
- **Bias:** None

## Greece (Pericles)
- **Ability — Hellenic League:** City-State Influence degrades half as slowly and recovers at twice the typical rate. Undocumented: City-State’s territory is considered friendly territory (No trespassing penalty and units heal at double rate).
- **Building — Odeon:** Replaces the Amphitheater. Available at Writing (instead of Drama and Poetry). Provides +4 Culture, +1 Gold, and does not require Gold maintenance. Houses a slot for a Great Writer specialist.
- **Unit — Hoplite:** Replaces Spearmen. 13 Strength.
- **Bias:** None (previously Mixed).

## Hittites (Muwatallis)
- **Ability — Bronze and Iron:** +1 Gold from Mines. This bonus increases to +3 gold when the mine is adjacent to at least 3 other mines. Start with Mining Technology.
- **Building — Lion’s Gate:** Replaces Walls. Cheaper to construct. In addition to the typical defensive perks, provides +2 Gold and +2 Culture in the City.
- **Unit — Heavy Chariot:** Replaces the Chariot Archer. Identical Ranged Strength and 3 Movement, but 10 Melee Strength. Gains a +20% defensive bonus when enemies initiate combat. Keeps ability on upgrade.
- **Bias:** River

## Hungary (András II)
- **Ability — Verszerzodes:** Walls, Castles, Arsenals and Military Bases provide +1 Production and Culture. May construct up to 2 Palaces.
- **Unit — Black Arquebusier:** Replaces Musketmen. Identical strength, but is capable of setting up to perform Ranged attacks (similar to a Siege Unit). Arrives with Accuracy I (+15% bonus in Open terrain). Slightly cheaper to produce. (Despite Accuracy typically denoting a bonus for Ranged units only, this bonus and other similar bonuses will apply to both Melee and Ranged attacks from this Unit.) Promotions are kept on upgrade. Yay!
- **Building — Orszaggyules:** Replaces the Palace. In addition to the regular bonuses of a Palace, provides +25% faster Great Person generation and +10% Production towards buildings in the City. Receive this building in the original Capital. Upon the discovery of Mathematics, a second Orszaggyules may be constructed in another City (for 67 hammers); this City becomes the new Capital of the empire. The original Orszaggyules may be sold and reconstructed to move this designation back; the Capital is located wherever the last Orszaggyules was constructed.
- **Bias:** None

## Huns (Attila)
- **Ability — Scourge of God:** No longer starts with Animal Husbandry Technology. +1 Production from all Pastures. Raze Cities at double speed and borrow City names from rival Civilizations.
- **Building — Qara U’y:** Replaces the Stable. In addition to the typical perks, yields +1 Faith from Horses. Provides 2 Horse Resources for the empire upon completion.
- **Unit — Horse Archer:** Replaces the Chariot Archer. Greater Melee Strength, receives no movement penalty in Rough terrain, and arrives with Accuracy I (+15% bonus in Open terrain). Requires Horses to construct. Keep promotion on upgrade. Yay! (In Brave New World, this Unit did not require Horses.)
- **Bias:** Avoid Forest, Jungle

## Inca (Pachacuti)
- **Ability — Great Andean Road:** Units ignore terrain costs moving through Hills. No Gold maintenance is paid for tile improvements in Hills, and half the Gold is paid for improvements elsewhere.
- **Improvement — Terrace Farm:** May only be constructed in Hills. Provides +1 Food, increasing by an additional +1 Food for every adjacent Mountain tile. Like Farms, receives an additional +1 Food if adjacent to freshwater at Civil Service, otherwise receiving this bonus at Fertilizer if not adjacent to freshwater.
- **Unit — Slinger:** Replaces the Archer. 5 Melee Strength. The Slinger has a chance to withdraw from melee combat initiated by foes, avoiding some damage depending on the available Movement of the attacker and the amount of available tiles behind the Slinger. Keeps the ability on upgrade.
- **Bias:** Hill

## India (Gandhi)
