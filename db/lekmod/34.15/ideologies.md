# LEKMOD ideologies (34.15)

Freedom, Order and Autocracy tenets by level. Game logs identify tenets
by internal `POLICY_*` IDs (e.g. `POLICY_ECONOMIC_UNION` is the tenet
"Economic Union"); the ID is normally the upper-cased tenet name.

## Freedom Tenets

Allows construction of the Statue of Liberty.

### Level 1

- **Avant Garde:** +25% Great Person generation.
- **Capitalism:** +1 Local Happiness per Market, Bank and Stock Exchange.
- **Civil Society:** Specialists consume only half the normal amount of Food.
- **Covert Action:** Spies have double the chance to successfully rig City-State elections. Influence from doing so doubled. Gain one additional Spy.
- **Creative Expression:** +2 Culture and +2 Tourism from each Great Work.
- **Economic Union:** All cities gain +5% gold for each Trade Route your civilization has with a City State. + 2 Trade Routes.
- **Volunteer Army:** 3 units are maintenance-free. Receive 3 Foreign Legion units immediately. All Melee and Gunpowder units receive the Morale promotion (+15% Combat Bonus) for free.

### Level 2

- **Arsenal of Democracy:** 25% Production towards Bombers, Fighters, Helicopters and Nuclear Missiles. +25% City Strength. All Fighter units receive the Dog Fighting I promotion (+33% Combat Bonus when performing an Air Sweep).
- **Free Mind:** +33% Generation towards Great Artists, Great Writers, and Great Musicians. Zoos, Stadiums, and Broadcast Towers increase a City’s Gold output by 20%.
- **New Deal:** Landmarks and Great Person improvements provide +4 of the appropriate yield. All Great Person tile improvements yield +1 of their respective yield for every Great Person tile improvement adjacent to it. Sacred Groves produce +1 of the appropriate yields, but do not profit from the adjacency bonus themselves. They do count as a Great Person tile improvement for the adjacency bonus for other improvements.

  Developer Note: For example, if a Manufactory and 2 Holy sites are all planted adjacent to each other, the Manufactory would yield +2 Production and each Holy Site would yield +2 Faith.

- **Universal Suffrage:** Unhappiness from Specialists is halved. Golden Ages last 50% longer.
- **Universal Healthcare:** +50% Production towards Hospitals and Medical Labs, and they each yield +3 Science and +2 happiness.
- **Urbanization:** +2 Production and +2 Science from each Workshop, Windmill and Factory.

### Level 3

- **Media Culture:** +34% Tourism generated by Cities with a Broadcast Tower.
- **Treaty Organization:** +1 Production and Gold from Specialists
- **Space Procurements:** May buy Spaceship Parts with Gold.

## Order Tenets

Allows construction of the Kremlin.

### Level 1

- **Double Agents:** Spies twice as likely to capture enemy Spies attempting to steal a Technology.
- **Hero of the People:** +20% Great Person generation.
- **Party Leadership:** +1 Food, +1 Production, +1 Gold, +1 Science and +1 Culture per city.
- **Resettlement:** New Cities start with an extra 4 Population, 4 tiles, a Workshop, Granary, Monument, Library, and Aqueduct already built.
- **Skyscrapers:** Gold cost of purchasing Buildings reduced by 50%.
- **Socialist Realism:** +2 Local Happiness from each Monument. +100% Production towards Monuments.
- **Young Pioneers:** +1 Local Happiness from every Workshop, Factory, and Solar/Nuclear/Hydro Plant.

### Level 2

- **Academy of Sciences:** +1 Local Happiness from every Observatory, Public School, and Research Lab.
- **Cultural Revolution:** +50% Tourism to civilizations that have less happiness. Also starts a Golden Age. Also provides -50% unhappiness from ideology pressure.
- **Five-Year Plan:** +2 Production per City and +1 Production for every Mine and Quarry.
- **Patriotic War:** +10% Combat Bonus when fighting in Friendly territory and units in friendly territory have +1 movement.
- **People’s Army:** 100% Production bonus towards Armories and Military Academie and each provide +2 Science and +2 Culture.

  Workers' Faculties: Factories increase a City’s Science output by 25%. +100% Production towards Factories.

### Level 3

- **Command Economy:** +3 Production, +3 Science and +1 Gold from Strategic Resources, with the exception of Horses. Provides 2 Uranium and 4 of every other Strategic Resource in the capital.
- **Iron Curtain:** Internal Trade Routes provide +10 Food or Production and an additional +1 extra per Era after the Industrial Era.
- **Spaceflight Pioneers:** May finish Spaceship parts with Great Engineers. A Great Engineer and a Great Scientist appear outside the Capital. Great Engineers provide double production when using them to finish a Spaceship part.

## Autocracy Tenets

Allows construction of the Prora.

### Level 1

- **Elite Forces:** All Military units have +10% increased Combat Strength and gain an extra 10 HP when healing.
- **Futurism:** Receive 250 Tourism (on quick speed) immediately whenever a Great Musician, Writer, or Artist is born.
- **Fascism:** +12 Global happiness in the Empire.
- **Industrial Espionage:** Spies steal technologies twice at twice the normal rate.
- **Militarism:** +1 Local Happiness from every Barracks, Armory, and Military Academy.
- **Mobilization:** Gold cost of purchasing and maintaining units reduced by 25%.
- **United Front:** Militaristic City-States gift units twice as often when you are at war with a common foe.

### Level 2

- **Iron Fist:** All Military units earn Gold, Culture, and Science from Kills. All Non-Airplane Military units earn 50% more Experience from Combat.
- **Lightning Warfare:** +1 Movement for Great Generals, Armored Units, Gunpowder Units naval units and all civilian units.
- **Nationalism:** Barracks, Armories, and Military Academies increase a City’s Tourism output by 10%. All Military units earn 100% more Experience during combat in Friendly territory.
- **Police State:** +50% Production towards Courthouses, Constabularies, and Police stations, and they each provide +2 Local Happiness.
- **Third Alternative:** Quantity of Strategic Resources produced is increased by 100%. +5 Food and +5 Science in the Capital.
- **Total War:** +15% Production when building Military units and new Military units start with +15 Experience.

### Level 3

- **Clausewitz's Legacy:** Receive a +25% attack bonus to all Military Units for the first 50 turns after this policy is adopted.
- **Cult of Personality:** Gain up to +100% more Tourism depending on your military strength (Soldiers) relative to other players.
- **Gunboat Diplomacy:** Tributing a City-State increases the influence level with by 50 (rather than decreasing it), and no longer clears quests Your military forces are 50% more effective at intimidating City-States.
