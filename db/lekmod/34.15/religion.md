# LEKMOD religion (34.15)

Pantheons, founder/follower/enhancer beliefs and reformations. Game logs
identify beliefs by internal `BELIEF_*` IDs which do not always match the
displayed name (e.g. `BELIEF_WALLS` is shown as "Goddess of Protection");
confirmed IDs are annotated inline - match on the ID where present.

## Faith Purchasing

Faith costs to purchase units or buildings no longer increase as you advance in eras. Faith purchase costs are now rounded down to the nearest 5 (instead nearest 10). Inquisitor cost reduced by 40%.

## Pantheons

- **Altars of Worship:** +20% Production towards Ancient, Classical and Medieval Wonders
- **Ancestor Worship:** +2 Culture from Shrines
- **Dance of the Aurora:** +1 Faith from Tundra tiles
- **Desert Folklore:** +1 Faith from Desert tiles
- **Divine Judgement** (`BELIEF_DIVINE_JUDGEMENT`): +2 Faith from Barracks, Courthouses; +3 Happiness from the Palace
- **Earth Mother:** +1 Faith from Mines, +2 Faith from Iron
- **God of Craftsmen:** +1 Production in every City
- **God of the Open Sky:** +1 Culture from Pastures
- **God of the Sea** (`BELIEF_GOD_SEA`): +1 Faith and Culture from Fish, Whales, Crabs, Pearls, Coral, and Atolls
- **God of War:** +1 Food and +1 Production from Barracks
- **God-King** (`BELIEF_GOD_KING`): +1 Food, Production, Science, Gold, Culture, Faith, and Happiness in the Palace
- **Goddess of Festivals:** +1 Production and +1 faith from Sugar, Wine and Spices
- **Goddess of Love:** +1 Local Happiness in Cities with 3 or more Citizens
- **Goddess of Protection** (`BELIEF_WALLS`): +1 Faith and Culture from Walls; +50% City Bombardment strength
- **Goddess of the Fields:** +2 Faith from Cotton, Incense, Tea and Coffee
- **Goddess of the Hunt:** +1 Food from Camp improvements
- **Harvest Festival:** +1 Food from Wheat and Maize
- **Messenger of the Gods:** +1 Faith, +1 Gold and +1 Science in Cities with a City Connection (the Capital receives this bonus upon the completion of the first City Connection)
- **Mystic Rituals:** +1 Food and Culture from Silk, Dyes, Perfume and Tobacco
- **Ocean’s Bounty:** +1 Production from Fishing Boat improvements and Atolls
- **One with Nature:** +5 from Natural Wonders
- **Oral Tradition:** +1 Culture from Plantation improvements
- **Rain Dancing:** +1 Culture, +2 Faith from Lakes and Oases
- **Religious Idols:** +1 Faith and Culture from Gold, Silver and Copper
- **Religious Settlements:** +1 Food and +20% Border Expansion rate in every City
- **Rite of Spring:** +1 Culture from Deer and Bison
- **Ritual Sacrifice:** +2 Culture and +1 Gold from Ivory, Furs and Truffles
- **Sacred Path:** +1 Culture from Forests, Jungles, Hardwood, and Rubber
- **Seafood Rituals:** +1 Food, Culture and Faith from Fish
- **Spirit Animals:** +1 Faith from Horses, Deer and Bison
- **Spirit Trees:** +1 Food and Faith from Lumber Mill improvements
- **Starlight Guidance:** +1 Culture, Faith and Local Happiness from Lighthouses
- **Stone Circles:** +1 Production and +1 Faith from Quarries, +1 Faith from Marble, Obsidian
- **Sun God:** +1 Food from Bananas, Citrus, Olives, Coconuts, and Cocoa
- **Tears of the Gods:** +1 Faith and +2 Gold from Gems, Amber, Lapis Lazuli and Jade
- **Vision Quests:** +1 Gold and Local Happiness from Shrines
- **Work Spirituals:** +1 Faith from Plantation Luxuries and Bananas

Pantheons are founded after your Civilization accumulates 15 Faith or more. The cost to found a Pantheon no longer increases when other players found their own Pantheons. After selecting a Pantheon, your current and future Cities will receive its effects until a proper Religion becomes more dominant in that City. Pantheons do not exert religious pressure and will not spread on their own. When you found a Religion, your Pantheon will become part of it, allowing it to be spread to other Civilizations.

## Founder Beliefs

- **Ceremonial Burial:** +1 Global Happiness per 2 Cities following this Religion; +1 Local Happiness in the Palace
- **Church Property:** +1 Faith in and +2 Gold from each City following this Religion. (As the faith is local, this tenet may provide benefits to rival Civilizations. The gold will always benefit the founder.)
- **Dawah:** +3 Culture and +4 faith from the Palace
- **Messiah:** Great Prophets gain +25% Spreading strength and are earned with 25% less Faith
- **Missionary Zeal:** Missionaries cost 50% less Faith and may Spread Religion +1 extra time
- **Mithraea:** +1% Gold in the City for every follower of this Religion; maximum of +20%. (this bonus is Local and may provide benefits to rival Civilizations)
- **Promised Land:** Religion spreads twice as fast to friendly Cities and Cities that already follow this Religion. Yields 67 Gold (quick speed) upon the first time converting a City to your Religion. n now degrades 50% slower and recovers influence 100% faster.
- **Reliquary:** Awards 50 Faith (on quick) whenever you expend a Great Person
- **Salat:** +4 Production from the Palace
- **Tithe:** +1 Gold for every 4 followers of this Religion
- **World Church:** +1 Culture for every 6 Followers of this Religion
- **Zakat:** +8 Gold from the Palace

Founder Beliefs are bonuses chosen upon founding a Religion and (usually) exclusively benefit the Civilization who founded that Religion. Other players will not receive your Founder bonus. Assuming no other factors, your first Great Prophet will spawn after accumulating 200 Faith (consuming that Faith, instead of removing all faith you have), allowing you to found a Religion.

## Follower Beliefs

Follower Beliefs impact all Cities that follow the Religion, no matter the Civilization. One Follower Belief is chosen upon founding a Religion, and a second is chosen when Enhancing that Religion.

## Follower Beliefs

- **Cathedrals (Building):** +5 Culture; costs 100 Faith
- **Choral Music:** Temples provide +3 Culture
- **Devoted Elite:** +3 Production and Gold, but +1 Unhappiness in Cities with at least 10 Citizens
- **Devout Performers:** +1 Culture from Colosseums, +1 Culture from Circuses, +5 Local Happiness and +4 Culture from the Circus Maximus
- **Divine Inspiration:** +1 Faith and Culture from National, Natural and World Wonders (this bonus appears as a tile yield on Natural Wonders)
- **Feed the World:** +1 Food from Shrines, +2 Food from Temples
- **Followers of the Refined Crafts:** +1 Culture, +2 Gold and Faith from Mints, Gem Cutters, Breweries, Groceries, Censer Makers and Textile Mills
- **Gurdwaras (Building):** +1 Culture and Local Happiness; +2 Faith and Science for every Religion present in this City; costs 180 Faith
- **Guruship:** +2 Food and +1 Production and Gold in every City housing a Specialist
- **Holy Warriors:** Allows purchase of military Units with Faith
- **Liturgical Drama:** +2 Faith, +1 Gold and +1 Culture from Amphitheaters and Opera Houses
- **Mandirs (Building):** +1 Food and Production from Luxury Resource tiles; costs 180 Faith
- **Mosques (Building):** +2 Faith, +2 Culture and Local Happiness; costs 100 Faith
- **Pagodas (Building):** +2 Faith, +1 Culture and Local Happiness; +10% of Food is carried over after a Citizen is born in the City; costs 130 Faith
- **Peace Gardens:** +1 Production and +2 Gold from Water Mills; +1 Local Happiness from Gardens
- **Religious Art:** +5 Culture and +10 Tourism from the Hermitage
- **Religious Centers:** +1 Production, Gold, and Local Happiness from Temples
- **Religious Community:** +1% Production for every follower of this Religion in the City; maximum of +10%
- **Sacred Waters:** +2 Faith and +1 Food from Gardens; +1 Local Happiness in Cities on rivers
- **Synagogues (Building):** +2 Faith, +1 Production and Culture; +15% Production towards the construction of Buildings; costs 130 Faith
- **Viharas (Building):** +1 Faith and Local Happiness, +15% Gold in the City, +1 Merchant Slot; costs 100 Faith

Enhancer Beliefs are chosen after using a Great Prophet adjacent to or within a Holy City (the City where a Religion was created). Like Founder Beliefs, these bonuses exclusively benefit the Civilization that created the Religion. Assuming no other factors, your second Great Prophet will spawn after accumulating 260 more Faith (consuming that Faith). The Prophet used to Enhance a Religion does not need to be of the same Religion.

## Enhancer Beliefs

- **Defender of the Faith:** Founder’s units gain a +15% Combat Bonus when within the borders of a friendly City that follows this Religion. ('within the borders of a friendly city' means only 3 tiles around a city. Switching tiles to a city following this religion activates Defender of the Faith.)
- **Dharma:** +10 Science from the Grand Temple
- **Disciples:** +2 Gold and Culture from Great Person tile improvements
- **Hajj:** +3 Food, Production and Culture from the Palace
- **Jizya:** +6 Production and Gold from the Grand Temple
- **Just War:** Units gain a +20% Combat Bonus when within the borders of an enemy City that follows this Religion
- **Karma:** +8 Culture in the Holy City
- **Kotel:** +6 Local Happiness from the Palace
- **Pilgrimage:** Gain +4 Faith and +3 Culture for every foreign City that follows this Religion
- **Religious Troubadours:** +3 Faith for each Trade Route sent to foreign Cities; receive +1 Trade Route
- **Religious Unity:** Religion spreads to Friendly or Allied City-State twice as fast; gain +45 Resting Influence with City States following this Religion. In addition, influence on City-States following your religion
- **Sanctified Innovations:** +1 Production, Science and Faith from National Wonders. Also unlocks the ability to buy National Wonders with Faith. Base cost is 200 Faith, increasing by 50 for every City owned by the player (quick speed). Example 400 Faith for a national wonder on 4 cities.
- **Unity of the Prophets:** +4 Faith and +2 Production from Holy Sites; rival Inquisitors and Prophets reduce this Religion’s presence by 50% (instead of eliminating it)

Reformation Beliefs can only be accessed through the Reformation tenet of the Piety Social Policy tree. Some bonuses benefit exclusively the founding Civilization, and some will apply for all Cities that follow the Religion. The turn after adopting the tenet, you can choose a Reformation belief, assuming you have a Religion founded. The Religion does not need to be enhanced, and, if no Religion was founded at the time the Reformation tenet was adopted, the Belief can be chosen the turn after the Religion is founded.

## Reformation Beliefs

- **Apostolic Palace** (`BELIEF_APOSTOLIC_PALACE`): +5 Food, Production, Science, Gold, Culture and Faith from the Grand Temple
- **City of God:** +2 Food and 1 Science from Holy Sites. Grants a free Great Prophet. (This prophet can not be used to enhance your religion and disappears on capture.)
- **Indulgences:** +1 Food, Production, Gold, Culture, and Faith in Cities following this Religion
- **Houses of Worship:** Allows purchase of Art Halls, Writing Auditoriums, and Vocal Chambers with Faith.
  - **Art Hall (Building):** +1 Culture and +4 Faith, +3 Tourism
  - **Writing Auditorium (Building):** +2 Culture and +1 Science
  - **Vocal Chamber (Building):** +3 Culture and +1 Happiness, 1 Musician Specialist Slot
- **Jesuit Education:** Allows purchase of Libraries Universities, Public Schools, Observatories, and Research Labs with Faith (40, 140, 250, 170, and 420 Faith respectively)
- **Sacred Sites:** +2 Gold, Culture and Faith from buildings purchased with Faith
- **Swords into Plowshares:** +1 Food and +20% Growth in Cities following this religion
- **Work Ethic:** Allows purchase of Workshops, Factories, Windmills and Hydro Plants with Faith (130, 300, 210, and 340 Faith respectively.)
- **Underground Sect:** +1 Science for every 4 Followers of this Religion
